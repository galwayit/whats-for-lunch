# Google sign-in button assets

The light/dark `google_sign_in.png` buttons are unmodified Android @4x pill assets
from https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip,
downloaded 1 October 2026. Use only for Sign in with Google and preserve the aspect
ratio. The embedded font, gradient logo, colours and wording are Google's approved
branding: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
