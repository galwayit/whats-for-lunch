		27 heading AI recommendations, Value: 2
			28 text AI recommendations
		29 text AI recommendations are optional. Before sending data, the app asks for your permission. An individual request can include your craving, budget, food preferences and allergens, a summary of recent meals, location, and nearby places. A group suggestion can include the group name, time and area, your preferences, and previous group picks. Before you agree, the app names the active main and backup AI providers and links to their privacy policies. Providers can include Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or another named AI provider. This information goes through our Supabase server to the main provider; if it cannot return a usable response, the backup may receive the same information. Adding or changing a recipient requires new permission. You can decline or withdraw AI sharing from Profile. This stops future AI requests until you agree again; it cannot recall information already processed. Service usage records contain an account identifier, time, provider, model, token count, latency, success or failure status, fallback attempts, and quota reservations. We do not intentionally store AI prompts in those server records. Recommendation history and its request context are stored in the local journal.

		45 heading Invitation reports and blocking, Value: 2
			46 text Invitation reports and blocking
		47 text You can report shared invitation content, guest names and proposed changes, and block the person responsible. A report stores the selected reason and a copy of the reported content in a private support queue. It is not shared with the person reported or sent to an AI provider for moderation. Blocking stores account identifiers or a one-way representation of an anonymous invitation token so our server can enforce it. Anonymous blocking applies to that invitation on that device; sign in before blocking for account-wide protection across invitations and devices. Clearing browser or app data can remove anonymous protection. Contact support if you need to undo a block. Reports are kept while their group exists or until removed during account deletion. Deleting an account removes its reports, block relationships and report snapshots of its guest content. Support correspondence is handled separately so we can respond to your request.
